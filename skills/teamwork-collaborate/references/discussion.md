# Discussion: <subject>

Optional outline for the topic's current discussion. Adapt the headings to
its existing organization; supporting detail can stay at the linked sources.

## Current understanding

The intended outcome, settled choices and the active disagreement or
uncertainty, including what the user needs explained before choosing and what
the answer changes. The user's decisive wording, dated, sits beside the choice
it settled.

## Decision map

A tree or nested outline linking the goal's necessary parts, choices within
them, and their dependencies. Show the active branch and each node's current
status. A superseded, rejected or deferred alternative is one line with its
reason and return condition. Nodes are rewritten when decisions change.

## Active question

The concrete options, evidence and applicability, trade-offs and recommendation.
Show the unresolved premise and how different answers affect the proposal.
Include the evidence location and relevant conditions with the judgment it
informs, such as a paper's figure or a run's output, rather than a bibliography
detached from the choice.

## What follows from this judgment

The next investigation, discussion or authorized action and its connection
to this judgment. Link any resulting plan and the affected uses of the judgment.

## Sources

Where the evidence and superseded reasoning can be found when needed, such as
readouts, runs or commits. The current view above does not depend on them.
