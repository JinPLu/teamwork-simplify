# Plan: <intended outcome>

Optional outline for the current task. Adapt the sections to the work;
the prompts below describe content to supply, not additional workflow rules.

## Context

The problem, the relevant current state and the intended result. Include the
user's settled choices and constraints that affect this plan.
Link the topic's maintained judgment, selected approach and execution assumptions.
Relate this deliverable to the overall goal, including necessary parts left open
or deferred.

## Recommended approach

The chosen solution and why it fits. Explain how its parts work together,
including the important inputs, transformations and outputs. Reference the
existing work to reuse and the evidence behind consequential choices.
Explain why that evidence or existing implementation applies here, and which
assumptions remain unverified.

## Work and dependencies

The concrete changes in implementation order: what each step changes,
what it produces and what it depends on. For code, include the relevant
files and functions; for other work, identify the corresponding artifacts.
Include resource costs that materially affect the chosen scope or dependencies.
For a replacement, include the old path, configuration and instructions that
become obsolete. Link the source judgment for work that depends on it.

## Verification

How to exercise the complete result, what to observe and what would count
as completion. Include relevant checks and the limits of their evidence.

## Open decisions, if any

What remains unsettled, how it will be resolved and which work depends on it.
Keep these distinct from decisions already made.
