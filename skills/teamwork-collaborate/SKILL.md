# Teamwork Collaborate

Make the current understanding easy to correct and carry settled decisions
into the requested outcome. Re-evaluate the trigger when the request changes.

## Method

1. **Establish what this decision needs.** Build on verified conversation and
   project context. Identify the goal, settled constraints and rejected
   approaches, then read only to fill relevant gaps. Do not restart a survey.
2. **Investigate facts; ask for choices.** Resolve discoverable questions
   yourself. Group the remaining independent choices that would change the
   direction, scope or acceptance, and defer questions dependent on those
   answers. Skip questions already answered; do not extend an interview merely
   to complete a batch. Make routine judgments under stated assumptions.
3. **Make the trade-off understandable.** Present meaningfully different
   options and their important costs, assumptions and benefits. Recommend
   when the evidence distinguishes one; scale the detail to the decision.
   Keep user decisions, your recommendations and unverified assumptions distinct.
4. **Carry decisions forward.** Do not ask again about settled choices,
   rejections or authorization. If new evidence materially changes a premise,
   explain its effect and confirm only the affected decision. An independent
   read-only check of a consequential assumption is optional, never a gate.
5. **Deliver the requested outcome.** An assessment or a decision can finish
   the discussion. For a complex plan, explain the need, intended outcome and
   recommended approach, then the concrete changes, dependencies and how to
   verify the result end to end. Ground it in the relevant sources; name
   critical files and existing functions to reuse when applicable. Describe
   repeated changes once with representative paths. Keep it easy to scan
   without omitting the inputs, outputs and constraints an executor needs.
   Separate unresolved choices from settled decisions. Revise the current
   approach when feedback changes it, keeping the steps consistent. Shared
   writing paths and launch suggestions are useful only for a real handoff;
   execution scheduling belongs to the host.
6. **Close with what is settled, what remains open and the next authorized
   action.** Continue with authorized execution when the host permits it.
